Data Ingestion & Scraper Identifiers

  • •
    Telegram & Naver Finance:

    Apify Actor: apify/cheerio-scraper. Ingests Naver Finance discussion boards (finance.naver.com/item/board.naver) and public tech channels with 50,400+ Korean retail posts.

  • •
    LIHKG Discussion Boards:

    Apify Actor: claude_code_reviewer/lihkg-scraper-en. Scrapes Hong Kong retail financial forum category 5 with Cantonese colloquial slang parsing.

  • •
    Exa.com Neural News API:

    Endpoint: https://api.exa.ai/search with bilingual query matrix (Hangul for KRX, Traditional Chinese for HKEX) and text extraction.

  • •
    Market Data (1h OHLCV):

    Ingested via Yahoo Finance API across all 10 tickers through Nov 20 close (905 total hourly bars, accounting for KRX CSAT 1h delayed open on Nov 19).

CLI Reproduction Workflow

# 1. Ingest OOS Data (Nov 19-20) & Causal Alignment
python -m src.ingest.run --start-date 2026-11-09 --end-date 2026-11-20
# 2. Extract 38 Continuous Features & Cultural NLP
python -m src.features.run
# 3. Train Huber+LGBM MoE & Walk-Forward OOS Eval
python -m src.models.run --eval-oos

Execution parameters: 15 bps hurdle rate, 5 bps turnover slippage drag, rolling 24h volume profile KDE support/resistance levels, and deterministic position sizing.
